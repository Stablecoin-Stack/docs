# Support Stablecoin Stack

We are building open-source tools to enable **frictionless, on-chain stablecoin payments**.  
Our goal is to make crypto payments feel like traditional bank transfers — simple, reliable, and secure.

As an early-stage project, **community support is essential** to keep development moving.
